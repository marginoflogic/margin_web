Retail traders are consistently traumatized by the "false breakdown" because they view the market through the lens of geometry rather than logistics. When price breaches a major support level, retail psychology dictates that the structure has shifted, prompting them to sell the breakout. Simultaneously, traders who were long have their stop-losses triggered—resulting in another massive wave of forced selling.

To the institutional algorithm, this is not a structural shift. It is a carefully engineered liquidity sweep. The primary dealer requires this exact concentration of sell orders to execute a massive institutional buy order without destabilizing the asset. They push the price into the void solely to trigger the cascade.

The moment the required volume is absorbed and the dealer's book is balanced, the operation is complete. The price immediately aggressively reverts back into the range. The retail traders who sold the breakdown are instantly trapped, their positions instantly converting into the required liquidity to fuel the move higher.

Stop trading the shapes. Start trading the mechanics of the sweep.
