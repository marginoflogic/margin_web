Total closed trades – 41 (1467,1 pips)
- Profitable trades – 37 (90.2%) = 1662,5 pips*

- Average pips per trade – 45.0 pips

- Average trade duration – 6.61 days

\*Includes an exit at breakeven (+/- 3 pips)—this implies active position management.
